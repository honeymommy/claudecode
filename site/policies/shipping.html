Shipping Policy

We ship within the United States only: all 50 states plus APO/FPO addresses. We do not ship internationally.

Every order ships from partner warehouses located in the USA. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

Typical delivery time is 2–5 business days after shipment. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your package leaves the warehouse.

Orders with several items may arrive in separate packages.
