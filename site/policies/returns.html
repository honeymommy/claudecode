Return Policy

You have 30 days from delivery to return any unused item in its original packaging.

For hygiene reasons, opened nursing items, pacifiers and personal care products can't be returned.

Item damaged or wrong? Message us within 7 days with a photo and we'll replace it or refund you at no cost.

Returns must be shipped from a US address. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive the return.
