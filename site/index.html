Frequently asked questions

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship within 1–2 business days and arrive in 2–5 business days. Everything ships from US warehouses, and you'll get a tracking number as soon as your order leaves.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not at the moment. We ship to all 50 states, plus APO/FPO addresses.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Just message us and we'll send instructions.
