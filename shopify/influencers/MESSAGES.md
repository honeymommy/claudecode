# Messages pour contacter les influenceuses (en anglais)

Personnalise toujours la **première phrase** avec un vrai détail de son contenu : c'est ce qui fait répondre.

---

## 1. Premier message (DM Instagram / TikTok)

> Hi [Name]! 💛 I loved your [post/video about … — specific detail]. I'm the founder of Honey Mommy, a small shop of mom-picked essentials for pregnancy and newborns, shipped from the US.
>
> I'd love to send you a free [product that fits her content] to try — no strings attached. If you like it, we also have a partner program: your own code (10% off for your followers) and 10% commission on every sale.
>
> Would that interest you? 🐝

## 2. Relance (5 jours plus tard, une seule fois)

> Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried 😊 Happy to send you a little Honey Mommy gift whenever works for you!

## 3. Elle accepte

> Yay, thank you! 💛 Here's how it works:
> 1. Apply here (2 min): [UpPromote link]
> 2. Once approved, you'll get your code [NAME10] and your dashboard.
> 3. Pick your free product on honeymommy.com and use this one-time code at checkout: [GIFT-XXXX]
>
> Whenever you post, please add #ad or the "Paid partnership" tag. That's an FTC rule we both need to follow. Can't wait to see what you think!

## 4. Après sa première publication

> Thank you so much for the post, it's beautiful! 🥹 Your code [NAME10] is live. You can track every sale in your dashboard. Let me know if you'd like a product for your next video!

## 5. Elle passe au niveau Gold

> Huge congrats, [Name]: you just passed $1,000 in sales with Honey Mommy! 🎉 You're now a Gold partner: a free product of your choice every month, and we'll feature you on our Instagram. Thank you for being part of our story. 💛
