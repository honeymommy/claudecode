# Honey Mommy Partner Program — Terms

*À coller dans UpPromote → Settings → Registration form → Terms & conditions. Ce texte est un modèle : fais-le relire par un professionnel si ton programme grandit.*

---

**1. The program.** The Honey Mommy Partner Program ("Program") lets approved creators ("Partners") earn a commission on purchases made on honeymommy.com through their personal discount code or referral link.

**2. Eligibility.** Partners must be at least 18 years old and have a public social media account, blog or website related to pregnancy, motherhood, babies or family. Honey Mommy may approve or decline any application at its discretion.

**3. Commission.** Partners earn the commission rate shown in their dashboard (10% unless otherwise agreed) on the order subtotal actually paid by the customer, after discounts and excluding taxes, shipping and refunds. Commissions on orders that are cancelled, refunded or returned are reversed.

**4. Payment.** Commissions are approved 30 days after the order (after the return window) and paid monthly via PayPal once the approved balance reaches $25. Partners are responsible for providing a valid PayPal account and any tax information we are legally required to collect.

**5. Your discount code.** Your code gives your audience the discount shown in your dashboard. Codes cannot be combined with other offers. You may not use your own code or link for your own purchases, or post it on coupon or deal websites.

**6. Disclosure (required).** Under US Federal Trade Commission rules, you must clearly disclose your relationship with Honey Mommy in every post, story or video that promotes our products, for example "#ad", "#sponsored" or "Paid partnership with @honeymommy". The disclosure must be visible without clicking "more".

**7. Honest content.** Only share your genuine opinion of products you have used or reviewed. Do not make medical or health claims (for example "prevents", "cures", "treats", "doctor recommended"), and do not show unsafe use of any product (for example soft items in a sleeping infant's crib).

**8. Prohibited promotion.** No spam, fake reviews, paid search ads on the "Honey Mommy" brand name, misleading content, or content that is offensive or illegal.

**9. Use of content.** You allow Honey Mommy to reshare your posts about our products on our own channels and website, with credit to you. We will not use your content in paid ads without your separate permission.

**10. Ending the partnership.** Either party may end the partnership at any time. Honey Mommy may suspend a Partner and cancel unpaid commissions in case of fraud or breach of these terms. Earned, approved commissions are otherwise paid.

**11. Changes.** We may update these terms or commission rates with 14 days' notice by email.

**12. Independent relationship.** Partners are independent creators, not employees or agents of Honey Mommy.
