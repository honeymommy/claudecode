## About — titre : *Our story*

**Like a bee choosing the sweetest flowers 🐝**

Honey Mommy started with a simple idea: moms-to-be already have a thousand things on their minds. Researching every bib, towel and nursing pillow shouldn't be one of them.

So we do the sorting for you. We review products from trusted US suppliers and keep only what is useful, comfy and safe — then ship it straight to your door from US warehouses.

**What we promise**
- **Mom-picked, not mass-picked.** Every product is reviewed before it reaches our shop.
- **Safety first.** We don't sell car seats, sleep products or anything that belongs in a specialty store.
- **Fast & free.** Free shipping on every order, from US warehouses, usually delivered in 2–5 business days.
- **No-stress returns.** 30 days, no questions asked on unused items.

Motherhood is a flower that opens gently. We're here for every petal. 💛

## FAQ — titre : *Frequently asked questions*

**How long does shipping take?**
Orders ship from US warehouses within 1–2 business days and usually arrive in 2–5 business days. You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

**Is shipping really free?**
Yes, on every order, with no minimum.

**Do you ship outside the United States?**
Not at the moment. We ship to all 50 states and APO/FPO addresses.

**Why did my order arrive in several packages?**
Our products ship from different US warehouses so they reach you faster. Each package has its own tracking number.

**How do I use a discount code?**
Enter your code in the "Discount code" box at checkout. One code per order.

**Can I return an item?**
Yes. Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. See our Return Policy for the details.

**My item arrived damaged or wrong. What do I do?**
Email us within 7 days with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, at no cost to you.

**Are your products safe for my baby?**
We only work with suppliers who sell in the US market, and we avoid categories that require specialist advice (car seats, sleep products, carriers). Always follow the care label and supervise your baby during use.

**How can I contact you?**
Email hello@honeymommy.com or use the chat bubble on the site. We reply within 24 hours, Monday to Friday.

## Shipping Policy

- We ship to the **United States only** (all 50 states and APO/FPO addresses).
- **Free standard shipping** on every order.
- Orders are processed within **1–2 business days** and usually delivered within **2–5 business days** after shipping.
- All products ship from **warehouses located in the United States**. An order with several items may arrive in separate packages, each with its own tracking number.
- You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking as soon as each package ships.
- If your tracking hasn't updated for 7 days, or your package shows delivered but you can't find it, email us and we'll investigate with the carrier.

---

## Refund Policy

- You may return **unused items in their original packaging within 30 days** of delivery.
- For hygiene reasons, **opened** nursing, feeding and personal care items cannot be returned unless they are defective.
- **To start a return**, email hello@honeymommy.com with your order number. We'll send you a return authorization and the return address. Items sent back without authorization can't be processed.
- Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item is **damaged, defective or not what you ordered**: in that case we cover everything and send a replacement or a full refund.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 business days** after the return is received. Original shipping was free, so nothing is deducted.
- Items bought with a discount code are refunded at the price actually paid.
